# Assessment wording review — 5 October 2026

Reviewed prompt, option, answer-key and passage consistency across the12 active banks (288 questions) and two Russian drafts (48 questions). This is an editorial consistency review, not qualified teacher approval or psychometric CEFR calibration.

Corrections: six progress prompts claimed a narrowly avoided failure where the keyed sentence established only that intervention prevented failure. They now ask about intervention preventing failure. Five opening-day reading answers unnecessarily specified a time of day not supplied by the passage; those answers now identify the day only. Spanish progress C2 reading had an overlapping distractor about concrete effectiveness; it now has a distinct historical-reference distractor. Answer indices and scoring thresholds are unchanged. Russian drafts remain disabled.

Remaining teacher review concerns: C1/C2 labels are provisional; many higher-band distractors are visibly implausible and may overestimate competence. Parallel themes across languages are intentional but do not establish equivalent difficulty. Separate fixed placement/progress banks and repeated exposure are not calibrated longitudinal measures. Listening uses synthetic speech; actual device voice, pronunciation and accessibility checks remain pending. No finding here constitutes certification that every multilingual item has a unique defensible answer in all dialects.

It is not imported by the live catalog or student submission actions. Activation still requires teacher approval, version decisions, matching database language/version constraints and readiness RPCs, complete submission/privacy tests, and device playback checks. No environment flag can turn these drafts on in this change.
