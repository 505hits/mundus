# Russian placement and progress drafts

These 48 original draft questions are not imported into the application, offered to students, or used to store scores. Russian has two separate 24-item banks: four per provisional A1–C2 band, with 12 grammar, 6 reading and 6 listening items. Answer keys are staff review material, not browser payloads.

Before release, a qualified teacher must check every prompt, answer and distractor for grammatical correctness, natural wording and a single defensible answer. Check that listening answers follow the passage, reading passages test understanding rather than guessing, and higher-band items have credible distractors. The provisional band labels are not validated CEFR classifications: short passages and obvious distractors may underestimate difficulty. Revise, pilot and review score thresholds before enabling.

The separate progress draft uses different prompts and listening passages. It needs independent teacher review and piloting; structural separation does not prove equivalent difficulty or a calibrated measure of improvement. Audio is proposed text only; Russian ru-RU device playback remains untested. Future activation requires catalog, server validation and storage/readiness support plus ownership/privacy tests. No migration or feature activation accompanies this draft.

Hungarian is part of the current Mundus teaching offer, but no Hungarian automated assessment bank is enabled or staged here. Until a reviewed bank exists, level confirmation remains with the teacher.
